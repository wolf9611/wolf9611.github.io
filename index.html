About me

Physicist & astronomer

I am the first astronomer from Ubajara, Ceará, Brazil. I study the positions and brightness of small Solar System bodies, with a focus on measuring them against dense stellar backgrounds.

My path connects a degree in Physics in Sobral, a master’s in Astronomy in Rio de Janeiro, and teaching in Ceará’s public schools. I am also a technology and artificial intelligence enthusiast.

From physics to astronomy

2018 thesis

Undergraduate degree in Physics

Universidade Estadual Vale do Acaraú (UVA) · Sobral, Ceará

I earned a Licenciatura in Physics, an undergraduate degree that includes teacher education. My thesis, Astronomia de Posição, explores celestial coordinates, spherical trigonometry, and the geometry used to locate objects in the sky.

2025 thesis

Master’s degree in Astronomy

Observatório Nacional (ON/MCTI) · Rio de Janeiro

My master’s research investigated astrometry and photometry of small Solar System bodies in crowded fields. Using observations of (10199) Chariklo, I studied how difference image analysis can improve measurements when neighboring stars interfere.

Teaching & public service

I teach Physics in the Ceará state public-school network, through SEDUC-CE. My work in education accompanies my academic training in Physics and Astronomy.

I passed the SEDUC-CE 2018 competitive examination for Physics teachers. My name appears in the final list of approved candidates published on 30 December 2019.

Current educational projects

These projects come from the daily work of a public-school Physics classroom.

Chamada

Records presence, absence, and excused absence on a phone for regrouped classes, gathers each student by their original class, and synchronizes the records with spreadsheets.

BuscApp

A project under my guidance for attendance monitoring, outreach to absent students, and communication with families. Emanuel Lázaro, a Computer Engineering student at the Federal University of Ceará (UFC), Sobral campus, collaborates directly.

Teaching materials

Lesson notes, presentations, and activities for secondary Physics and ENEM preparation, plus simulations and Python animations of the work done by a gas, electrical circuits, charged-particle motion in magnetic fields, and the celestial sphere.

Astronomy, technology & AI

I am beginning doctoral studies along the same research trajectory, with a focus on astrometric software that integrates machine learning and artificial intelligence. I am interested in making measurements of small bodies more reliable in challenging stellar fields.
