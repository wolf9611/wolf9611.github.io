Research

My work centers on precise measurements of small Solar System bodies and on building the next generation of astrometric tools — including machine learning and AI — for crowded fields and survey-scale data.

Master’s research (Observatório Nacional)

At ON I developed and applied methods for astrometry and photometry of small bodies in dense stellar fields. Crowding challenges centroiding, trailing fits, and photometric calibration; the goal is reliable positions and fluxes for orbit determination and physical studies. The dissertation is available via the ON thesis repository (see Publications).

(10199) Chariklo and centaur science

Chariklo is a benchmark centaur with a ring system revealed by stellar occultations. It is a natural laboratory for linking high-precision astrometry, multi-band photometry, and dynamical context. My MSc work engaged this science case within the Brazilian astrometric ecosystem (PRAIA / DIA-related workflows).

PhD direction — astrometric code with ML & AI

I am starting a PhD in the same research line, with a primary focus on developing astrometric software that uses machine learning and artificial intelligence. Intended themes include:

  • Learning-based or hybrid methods for source detection and centroiding under crowding and complex PSFs;
  • Robust trailing and moving-object measurement on survey cadences;
  • Reproducible, scalable pipelines that connect classical astrometry to modern ML tooling;
  • Applications to centaurs, TNOs, and other small-body populations in wide-field imaging.

Education & outreach interface

As a secondary-school Nature Sciences teacher in Ceará, I also care about translating survey astronomy and computational methods into teachable narratives — without diluting the underlying science.
