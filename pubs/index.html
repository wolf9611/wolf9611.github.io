Thesis

Astrometria e fotometria de pequenos corpos do Sistema Solar em imagens com campos estelares densos
Josué da Silva Cavalcante · Master’s dissertation · Astronomy · Observatório Nacional (ON), Rio de Janeiro · 2025
Advisor: Dr. Julio I. Bueno de Camargo · Co-advisor: Dr. Adriano Pieres
Difference image analysis (DIAPL2) on SOAR/SOI imaging of (10199) Chariklo in a dense field; PRAIA reference-star astrometry; improved residuals vs. NIMA (e.g. 0.066″ → 0.018″ in ∆α cos δ); rotational light curve with σφ = 0.0025.
