Master’s thesis

Astrometria e fotometria de pequenos corpos do Sistema Solar em imagens com campos estelares densos
Josué da Silva Cavalcante · M.S. thesis · Astronomy · Observatório Nacional (ON), Rio de Janeiro · 2025
Supervisor: Dr. Julio I. Bueno de Camargo · Co-supervisor: Dr. Adriano Pieres
Difference image analysis (DIAPL2) applied to SOAR/SOI imaging of (10199) Chariklo in a dense field; PRAIA reference-star astrometry; reduced residuals versus NIMA (e.g., 0.066″ → 0.018″ in ∆α cos δ); rotational light curve with σφ = 0.0025.
